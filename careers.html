Tool & Die Maker

Full-TimeToolmaker

Toolmakers build and refine custom tooling in a shop where project variety, precision, and practical judgment matter every day.

Responsibilities

  • Read and interpret blueprints, CAD drawings, and schematics
  • Operate drills, lathes, grinders, mills, and related machine tools
  • Set up machines and test programs
  • Inspect finished components and assemblies
  • Manage multiple component projects

Requirements

  • Journeyman status or certification preferred
  • Ability to read and interpret blueprints and schematics
  • Knowledge of machine tools
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Reliability and critical thinking skills

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly and salary rates
  • Healthcare insurance coverage
  • 401(k) with company matching
  • Paid time off and holidays
  • Training and growth opportunities

How to apply

Send your resume and completed application to hr@zemantool.com.

CNC Machinist

Full-TimeMachinist

CNC machinists support precision machining work by setting up, programming, checking, and maintaining equipment for custom parts and tooling.

Responsibilities

  • Compare sample parts to CAD models and drawings
  • Program using G-code
  • Set up machines and test programs
  • Confirm parts are cut to specification
  • Create programming and setup instructions as needed
  • Perform regular machine maintenance

Requirements

  • CNC background or certification preferred
  • Ability to read and interpret blueprints
  • Strong communication skills
  • Self-responsibility and reliability
  • Ability to multitask and solve problems

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly and salary rates
  • Healthcare insurance coverage
  • 401(k) with company matching
  • Paid time off and holidays
  • Training and growth opportunities

How to apply

Send your resume and completed application to hr@zemantool.com.
