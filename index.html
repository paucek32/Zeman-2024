About Zeman

Family-built precision with a modern manufacturing floor.

Founded in Waukesha in 1966, Zeman Tool & Manufacturing has grown from a small machine shop into a precision tooling partner for customers across the Midwest, the United States, and global manufacturing programs.

The company culture is grounded in trust, practical problem solving, and durable work. That shows up in the way projects move through engineering, machining, assembly, inspection, and final delivery.

Aerospace Tooling

Foundry Tooling
